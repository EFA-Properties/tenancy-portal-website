How it works

Set up in about ten minutes

If you can send an email, you can use Tenancy Portal.

  1. 01Send

    Add your property and tenant, then upload their documents: gas safety record, EPC, EICR, How to Rent guide, deposit certificate and anything else you share.

  2. 02Track

    Your tenant gets an email with a sign in link. See when each document has been sent, opened and acknowledged.

  3. 03Keep a record

    Every acknowledgement is dated and saved with a signed copy, so your records stay organised in one place.

Features

What you get

Easy sign in for tenants

No passwords to forget. Tenants click a link in their email and they are in, on any phone.

Dated confirmations

Each confirmation is logged with the date, time and IP address.

Expiry reminders

A nudge before certificates run out, so gas safety and EICR renewals do not slip.

Maintenance requests

Tenants report problems through the portal and every report is dated, which helps with Awaab's Law.

Deposit records

Note which scheme protects each deposit and send the certificate through the portal.

Audit report

Every action is recorded. Download a full report for any property whenever you need it.

Inventories

Room by room inventories with photos, signed off by the tenant online.

Rule change heads ups

Plain English updates when landlord rules change, showing which of your properties are affected and when.
